THE SYSTEM, EXPLAINED

FOUR PARTS. ONE PURPOSE.

Confidence.
Broken down.

From pedal pressure to a controlled stop.
Scroll to see how each part works.

01 / CALIPER

Pressure becomes grip.

The caliper’s pistons push the pads against the rotor. A rigid body helps keep that pressure even.

02 / BRAKE PADS

Friction does the work.

Pad material grips the rotating disc. The compound affects bite, noise and how braking feels as temperatures change.

03 / ROTOR

Motion becomes heat.

The rotor absorbs energy as the pads grip. Internal vents move air through the disc to carry heat away.
